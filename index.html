The new SignStix media player, which runs SignFusion.

Important: SignFusion displays digital signage content alongside an existing TV feed. Partners and their customers are responsible for checking the terms of their TV provider and broadcast subscription, including any restrictions on public or commercial viewing and on overlaying or altering broadcast content. In our testing, SignFusion showed no noticeable delay compared with a TV showing the same feed directly, though results can vary with the venue’s TVs, splitter and TV source. Where timing matters, such as live sport, we recommend discussing the setup with your SignStix Account Manager before installation.
